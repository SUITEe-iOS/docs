SUITEe automates the entire guest journey by connecting to your existing Property Management System (PMS) and driving smart devices in your property. Here's how it all works, from start to finish.

## The big picture

SUITEe sits between your **PMS** (where bookings happen) and your **smart devices** (locks, thermostats, sensors). It reads booking data, prepares rooms, generates access credentials, and manages energy — all without manual intervention.

## The six-step flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Booking Made">
    A guest books a room through any channel — your website, Booking.com, Airbnb, or any other platform. The booking is recorded in your **PMS** (Property Management System).

    <Info>
      SUITEe works with bookings from **any** channel. As long as the booking reaches your PMS, SUITEe will pick it up.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="PMS Sends Data to SUITEe">
    Your PMS automatically sends the booking details to SUITEe:
    - Guest name and contact information
    - Room/unit assignment
    - Check-in and check-out dates and times
    - Reservation status (confirmed, modified, or cancelled)

    This happens **automatically** — no manual data entry is needed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="SUITEe Prepares Everything">
    Once SUITEe receives the booking data, it automatically:
    - **Creates a digital key or PIN code** for the guest
    - **Prepares the room climate** — pre-heating or pre-cooling to a comfortable temperature
    - **Sends check-in instructions** to the guest via email or SMS

    <Tip>
      The guest receives instructions before arrival, just like airline check-in. They can complete online registration in advance so there's zero wait time at the property.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Guest Arrives and Checks In">
    When the guest arrives at the property:
    1. They open the **Guest Web App** on their smartphone (no download needed — it works in the browser)
    2. They complete any remaining registration steps
    3. They use their **mobile key** or **PIN code** to open the building entrance
    4. They proceed directly to their room and unlock the door

    **No reception desk, no waiting, no physical keys.**
  </Step>

  <Step title="During the Stay">
    While the guest is staying:

    | What Happens | How It Works |
    | :--- | :--- |
    | **Room temperature** | Guest controls it from their phone via the Guest Web App |
    | **Door access** | Guest locks/unlocks with their mobile key or PIN |
    | **Room empty** | Sensors detect vacancy → system reduces heating/cooling to save energy |
    | **Window opened** | Window sensor detects it → heating/cooling pauses automatically |
    | **Amenity access** | Guest can access gym, spa, pool if included in their booking |

    Staff can monitor everything in real-time from the **Admin Dashboard** — room status, device health, alerts, and energy consumption.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check-Out">
    At the scheduled check-out time, SUITEe automatically:
    - **Expires the guest's mobile key and PIN code** — they can no longer open the room
    - **Switches the room to "Eco Mode"** — minimum energy usage
    - **Notifies housekeeping** — the dashboard shows the room is ready to clean
    - **Resets the room** for the next guest

    <Info>
      The next guest's access credentials are generated automatically when their check-in time approaches. The cycle repeats seamlessly.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Key takeaway

The entire flow — from booking to check-out — runs on **auto-pilot**. Staff only need to intervene when something needs manual attention (which the dashboard alerts you about). This is how SUITEe eliminates the need for a 24/7 reception desk.
