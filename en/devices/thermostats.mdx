Smart thermostats are the heart of SUITEe's energy automation. They control heating and cooling in each room, automatically adjusting based on occupancy, reservations, and sensor data.

## What are smart thermostats?

SUITEe's smart thermostats are digital climate controls that:
- **Automatically adjust temperature** based on whether a room is occupied or empty
- Can be **controlled remotely** by staff from the dashboard
- Allow **guests to adjust** their room temperature via the Guest Web App
- **Pre-heat or pre-cool** rooms before a guest arrives

---

## Energy modes

Every room with a smart thermostat operates in one of two modes:

| Mode | Temperature | When It's Active | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Guest Mode** | Comfortable (typically 20–24°C / 68–75°F) | When a guest is checked in | Maximum comfort for the guest |
| **Eco Mode** | Energy-saving (typically 15–18°C / 59–64°F) | When the room is vacant | Minimum energy consumption |

The switch between modes happens **automatically** based on:
- Reservation data (check-in/check-out times)
- Occupancy sensor readings (is someone in the room?)
- Window sensor status (is a window open?)

---

## How temperature automation works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Before guest arrival">
    Room stays in **Eco Mode** (minimal energy). Shortly before the guest's check-in time, the thermostat begins **pre-conditioning** — heating or cooling to a comfortable temperature so the room is ready when the guest walks in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Guest is in the room">
    Room operates in **Guest Mode**. The guest can adjust the temperature:
    - Via the **Guest Web App** on their phone
    - Some thermostats also have physical controls on the device

    Staff can also adjust temperature remotely from the dashboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Guest leaves temporarily">
    The **occupancy sensor** detects the room is empty. After a configurable delay (e.g., 15–30 minutes), the thermostat reduces heating/cooling to save energy — but doesn't go full Eco Mode, since the guest is still checked in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Guest returns">
    The occupancy sensor detects the guest's return. The thermostat **quickly restores** the comfortable temperature.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Window is opened">
    The **window sensor** detects an open window. Heating or cooling **pauses immediately** to avoid wasting energy. When the window is closed, normal operation resumes.

    <Tip>
      This single automation can save significant energy. In winter, guests often open windows for fresh air while the heating runs full blast — the window sensor prevents this waste.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Guest checks out">
    The room immediately switches to **Eco Mode**. It stays at minimum energy until the next guest's pre-conditioning period begins.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Controlling thermostats from the dashboard

Staff can manage thermostats across the entire property from the dashboard:

| Action | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **View all temperatures** | See current temperature readings for every room |
| **Adjust temperature** | Set a specific temperature for any room remotely |
| **Switch mode** | Manually switch between Guest Mode and Eco Mode |
| **View history** | See temperature changes over time for any room |
| **Check device status** | Verify the thermostat is online and functioning |

---

## Guest temperature control

Guests can adjust their room temperature from the **Guest Web App** on their smartphone:
- No app download needed — works in the phone's browser
- Simple interface with temperature up/down controls
- Changes take effect within minutes

<Info>
  Guest temperature adjustments are **limited to a reasonable range** to prevent extreme settings. The exact range is configurable by the property manager.
</Info>
