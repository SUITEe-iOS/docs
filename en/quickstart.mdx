This guide walks you through the fastest path to getting started with SUITEe. By the end, you will have logged in, oriented yourself around the dashboard, and understood the key areas of the platform.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, make sure you have:

- **Login credentials** (email and password) provided by the SUITEe team or your property administrator
- A **modern web browser** — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest version recommended)
- A **stable internet connection**

<Info>
  If you haven't received your login credentials, contact your property manager or the SUITEe support team at [suite-e.com/contact-us](https://suite-e.com/contact-us/).
</Info>

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the SUITEe platform">
    Open your web browser and navigate to **[hotel.suite-e.com](https://hotel.suite-e.com/)**. You will see the login screen with the SUITEe logo, an email field, and a password field.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/login-german.png" alt="SUITEe login page showing email and password fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your credentials and log in">
    1. Enter your **email address** in the email field
    2. Enter your **password** in the password field
    3. Click the **"Log In"** button

    <Tip>
      SUITEe supports both **English (EN)** and **German (DE)**. You can switch languages using the language selector on the login page — look for the flag icon or language abbreviation.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete OTP verification (if prompted)">
    For added security, SUITEe may ask you to enter a **One-Time Password (OTP)**:

    1. Check your email or phone for a 4–6 digit code
    2. Enter the code in the verification field
    3. Click **"Verify"**

    <Warning>
      OTP codes expire after a few minutes. If the code has expired, click **"Resend Code"** to get a new one.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Explore the Admin Dashboard">
    Once logged in, you will land on the **Admin Dashboard** — your central control hub. Here's what you'll find:

    | Dashboard Section | What It Shows |
    | :--- | :--- |
    | **Today's Check-ins/Check-outs** | Guests arriving and departing today |
    | **Room Status** | Which rooms are occupied, vacant, or being cleaned |
    | **Device Health** | Status of all smart devices (locks, thermostats, sensors) |
    | **Recent Alerts** | Issues needing attention (low battery, offline devices) |
    | **Energy Overview** | Energy consumption summary across rooms |

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/dashboard-overview.png" alt="SUITEe admin dashboard showing key metrics and room status" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your preferences">
    Customise your experience:

    - **Dark Mode / Light Mode** — Toggle between visual themes based on your preference
    - **Language** — Switch between English and German at any time
    - The system remembers your choices across sessions
  </Step>
</Steps>
