## About SUITEe

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is SUITEe?">
    SUITEe is a smart hotel management platform that automates **access** (digital keys and PIN codes) and **energy** (heating, cooling, lighting) for hospitality businesses. It connects to your existing PMS to run your property on auto-pilot — without needing a 24/7 reception desk.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who is SUITEe for?">
    SUITEe is designed for **hotels, serviced apartments, vacation rentals, wellness properties, and multi-property owners** — any hospitality business that wants to reduce costs, save energy, and improve the guest experience.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does SUITEe replace my existing booking system (PMS)?">
    **No.** SUITEe works **alongside** your PMS, not as a replacement. You continue to manage bookings, pricing, and billing in your PMS. SUITEe reads booking data from your PMS and automates access, energy, and guest communications.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Smart devices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What smart devices does SUITEe support?">
    SUITEe works with:
    - **Digitally controlled door locks** (smart locks)
    - **Smart thermostats** for heating/cooling control
    - **Light and switch modules** for smart lighting
    - **Various sensors** — occupancy/presence, window, temperature, motion, and water sensors
    - **Gateways** — communication hubs connecting devices to the cloud

    All devices can be centrally controlled and automated from the dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can SUITEe use existing smart devices from other brands?">
    Generally **no**. SUITEe uses its own certified devices to ensure reliable operation and security. The devices are designed to work together as a unified ecosystem.

    However, you can choose to install **only certain components** — for example, only the door locks, or only the thermostats. You don't have to install everything at once.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long do smart lock batteries last?">
    Smart lock batteries typically last **several months** under normal usage. The dashboard monitors battery levels and alerts you when a battery is getting low, giving you time to replace it before it runs out.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security and reliability

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How secure is SUITEe?">
    Very secure. SUITEe provides multiple layers of security:
    - All devices are **certified to industry standards**
    - Communication is **encrypted** and protected against common attacks
    - The software is fully **GDPR-compliant** (European data protection)
    - The **hybrid design** means the system stays operational even if individual parts fail
    - Every door access is **logged** with an audit trail
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens if there is no Wi-Fi or a power outage?">
    Operations remain stable:
    - Guests can still **unlock doors using their PIN code** — PINs work offline
    - Smart locks work on **battery power** — they don't need internet or mains power
    - All essential devices can be **operated manually** as a fallback
    - When power and internet are restored, everything **reconnects and syncs automatically**
    - **No data is lost**
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can guests still access their room during an internet outage?">
    **Yes.** Smart locks work independently of internet connectivity. Guests can always use their **PIN code** to open their door, even if the Wi-Fi is down. Mobile keys may not work during an outage (since they rely on the cloud), but PIN codes always work.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if a guest loses their phone or forgets their PIN?">
    Staff can **generate a new access code at any time** from the dashboard. The old code is immediately invalidated. As a final backup, all locks also have a **manual key override** — a physical keyhole that staff can use.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Installation

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How quickly can SUITEe be installed?">
    You receive **pre-configured devices** that just need to be physically mounted. Installation takes about **1 hour per room** on average. Your existing staff (technicians or electricians) can handle the installation.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to renovate my property?">
    **No.** SUITEe is a **retrofit solution** — devices are designed to replace or fit into existing hardware. No construction, no rewiring, no expensive renovations. Smart locks replace old locks, thermostats replace old thermostats, and sensors attach with adhesive or screws.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Who does the installation?">
    Your existing maintenance team, electricians, or technicians can handle the installation. The SUITEe team provides pre-configured devices and guidance. Once mounted, devices automatically appear in the dashboard.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## PMS and integration

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which PMS systems does SUITEe work with?">
    SUITEe currently supports:
    - **Mews** — A popular cloud-based hotel management system
    - **Apaleo** — A modern, open hospitality platform
    - **Clock+** — A hotel management and POS system

    More integrations are being added. If your PMS is not listed, [contact the SUITEe team](https://suite-e.com/contact-us/) to discuss integration possibilities.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to enter booking data into SUITEe manually?">
    **No.** The PMS integration is automatic. Bookings created in your PMS are automatically synced to SUITEe. Changes and cancellations also sync automatically. You never need to enter data twice.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Guest experience

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does the guest need to download an app?">
    **No.** The Guest Web App works directly in the phone's **web browser**. No app store download is needed. The guest simply clicks a link from their email/SMS, and the app loads instantly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I manage multiple properties?">
    **Yes.** SUITEe is built for **multi-property management**. You can view and control all your properties from a single dashboard. Switch between properties without logging out.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does SUITEe save energy?">
    Smart sensors detect when rooms are empty and **automatically reduce heating/cooling**. When a window is opened, the system **pauses climate control**. Rooms switch to **Eco Mode** immediately after check-out. This prevents energy waste from running systems in unoccupied rooms — the savings compound across your entire property.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can guests control their room temperature?">
    **Yes.** Guests can adjust their room temperature from the **Guest Web App** on their phone. Changes take effect within minutes. Temperature adjustments are limited to a reasonable range set by the property manager.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
