SUITEe is a **smart hotel management platform** that helps hotels, serviced apartments, vacation rentals, and other hospitality businesses run their operations automatically — without needing a 24/7 front desk or reception staff.

> **"Your Hospitality Business. Smarter. On Auto-Pilot."**

![SUITEe Smart Hotel Platform](/images/suite-e-hero.png)

SUITEe does two main things:

1. **Access Automation** — Replaces physical keys with digital mobile keys and PIN codes that are created and expired automatically.
2. **Energy Automation** — Automatically controls heating, cooling, and lighting based on whether a room is occupied or empty.

The platform comes with both **software** (the admin dashboard and guest web app) and **hardware** (smart locks, thermostats, and sensors installed in rooms).

<Info>
  SUITEe does **not** replace your existing booking system (PMS). Instead, it **connects to your PMS** and uses booking data to automate everything else — access, energy, and guest communications.
</Info>

## Who is SUITEe for?

| Business Type | How SUITEe Helps |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hotels & Motels** | Remove the need for night reception; guests check in anytime |
| **Serviced Apartments** | Automate access for self-service stays |
| **Vacation Rentals** | Remote management with no on-site staff needed |
| **Wellness & Spa Properties** | Control access to specific areas (spa, gym, pool) |
| **Multi-Property Owners** | Manage several properties from one central dashboard |
