SUITEe lets you personalize your platform experience with language preferences, visual themes, quick links shortcuts, and profile management. Your preferences are saved automatically across sessions.

## Top navigation & header controls

The top navigation header provides quick access to core account tools and platform utility features:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings-language-menu.png" alt="Header navigation controls showing notification counter, quick links icon, and language selector dropdown" />
</Frame>

### 1. Notifications
Located in the header controls area, the **notification bell** displays an unread counter badge (e.g. `48`). Clicking the notification bell opens your recent system alerts, property updates, and guest activity notifications.

### 2. Quick Links (Schnellzugriff)
Directly adjacent to the notification bell is the **Quick Links** icon (grid of circles with a plus symbol). Clicking this button opens the **Schnellzugriff** shortcut popup:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings-quick-links.png" alt="Schnellzugriff (Quick Links) popup menu with shortcuts to devices, property, and email configuration" />
</Frame>

The Quick Links menu gives property managers one-click shortcuts to key operational areas:
- **Öffentliche Geräte** — Manage public and shared property devices
- **Immobilie konfigurieren** — Configure property settings and room configurations
- **E-Mail-Dienst konfigurieren** — Manage email notification services and SMTP setups

### 3. Language Selector (Sprachauswahl)
The **language selector** button displays your active language code (e.g., `DE` or `EN`). Clicking it opens the language selection dropdown:

| Language | Code | Native Name |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **English** | EN | English |
| **German** | DE | Deutsch |
| **Polish** | PL | Polski |
| **Romanian** | RO | Română |

<Info>
  Selecting a language updates the entire dashboard interface — navigation menus, form fields, action buttons, and system messages — instantly.
</Info>

### 4. User Profile Menu
Clicking your account avatar in the sidebar or header menu opens the user account options:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings-user-menu.png" alt="User profile menu with Profil link, Dunkler Modus toggle, and Abmelden option" />
</Frame>

The user menu provides:
- **Profil** — Opens your full account profile page (`/profile`)
- **Dunkler Modus** — Toggle switch to instantly toggle Dark Mode on or off
- **Abmelden** — Safely sign out of your SUITEe session

## Visual theme (Dunkler Modus)

SUITEe supports both dark and light visual themes to suit your work environment:

- **Dark Mode (Dunkler Modus)** — Ideal for low-light environments, night shifts, and reducing eye strain
- **Light Mode** — Best for bright office environments and daytime use

### How to toggle dark mode
1. Click on your user profile menu at the bottom of the sidebar
2. Toggle the **Dunkler Modus** switch
3. The interface theme switches instantly and remembers your setting across logins

## Profile management (`/profile`)

Access your profile settings by clicking **Profil** in the user menu or navigating to `https://hotel.suite-e.com/profile`.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings-profile-personal.png" alt="SUITEe Profile page showing profile picture upload badge and Persönliche Informationen section in German" />
</Frame>

### Profile Picture Upload
At the top of the profile page, your account display avatar features a green **camera icon badge**.
- **Uploading an Image**: Click the camera icon on your avatar to select and upload a profile photo from your device.
- **Display**: Your updated picture will appear in the top navigation bar, sidebar, and throughout team activity logs.

### Personal Information (Persönliche Informationen)
The **Persönliche Informationen** section displays your core identity and contact details:

| Field | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Name** | Your full display name (e.g. `dolly arc 123`) |
| **E-Mail-Adresse** | Your registered email address used for sign-in and system notifications |
| **Telefonnummer** | Contact phone number linked to your manager profile |
| **Benutzerrolle** | Your assigned platform role and access privileges (e.g., Property Admin with full module access) |

To update any of these details, click the **Bearbeiten** (Edit) button in the upper right corner of the **Persönliche Informationen** card.

### Password Security (Passwort)
The **Passwort** card lets you securely update your account password:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/settings-profile-password.png" alt="Passwort section on the SUITEe profile page in German" />
</Frame>

- **Current Status**: Masked view showing `Neues Passwort` (`••••••••`).
- **Changing Password**: Click the **Bearbeiten** (Edit) button on the **Passwort** card, enter your current password followed by your new password, and save your changes.

## Session management & security

- **Session Timeout** — For security, inactive sessions time out after an extended period of inactivity. Simply sign back in to resume your work.
- **Multiple Devices** — You can stay signed in across multiple workstations or mobile devices simultaneously.
- **Secure Logout (Abmelden)** — Always click **Abmelden** in the user menu when using shared or public computers.
