title: "Forgot Password"
description: "How to reset your SUITEe password if you've forgotten it — step-by-step walkthrough of the password reset flow."
---

If you've forgotten your SUITEe password, you can reset it quickly using the password recovery flow. The system will send a reset link to your registered email address.

## How to reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the login page">
    Navigate to **[https://hotel.suite-e.com/auth](https://hotel.suite-e.com/auth)** in your browser (or go directly to **[https://hotel.suite-e.com/auth/forgot-password](https://hotel.suite-e.com/auth/forgot-password)**). You will see the standard login page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click 'Passwort vergessen?'">
    Below the password field on the login page, click the **"Passwort vergessen?"** ("Forgot password?") link (or go directly to [https://hotel.suite-e.com/auth/forgot-password](https://hotel.suite-e.com/auth/forgot-password)). You will see the **Passwort zurücksetzen** screen:

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/forgot-password-german.png" alt="SUITEe German Reset Password screen" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    On the password reset screen, enter the **email address** associated with your SUITEe account into the **E-Mail** field. If the field is submitted blank, the platform displays an immediate validation warning:

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/forgot-password-german-validation.png" alt="Validation error when required email field is blank" />
    </Frame>

    <Info>
      Make sure to enter the same email address that was used to create your SUITEe account. If you're unsure which email is linked, contact your property administrator.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click 'E-Mail verifizieren'">
    Click the **"E-Mail verifizieren"** ("Verify Email") button. The system displays a confirmation banner confirming that the reset link has been sent to your inbox:

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/forgot-password-german-submitted.png" alt="Confirmation message after requesting password reset link" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check your email inbox">
    Open your email inbox and look for a message from SUITEe with the subject line containing "password reset" or similar.

    <Warning>
      If you don't see the email within a few minutes:
      - Check your **spam** or **junk** folder
      - Make sure you entered the correct email address
      - Try sending the reset link again
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the reset link">
    Open the email and click the **password reset link**. This will take you to a page where you can set a new password.

    <Warning>
      Reset links typically **expire after a limited time** (usually 30 minutes to 1 hour). If the link has expired, go back to step 2 and request a new one.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set your new password">
    1. Enter your **new password** in the "New Password" field
    2. **Confirm** the new password by typing it again
    3. Click **"Reset Password"** or **"Save"**

    <Tip>
      Choose a strong password that includes:
      - At least 8 characters
      - A mix of uppercase and lowercase letters
      - At least one number
      - At least one special character (e.g., `!`, `@`, `#`)
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log in with your new password">
    After a successful reset, you will be redirected to the login page. Enter your email and your **new password** to log in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How long does it take to receive the reset email?">
    The reset email usually arrives within **1–2 minutes**. If it takes longer, check your spam folder or try requesting a new reset link.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I reset my password from my phone?">
    Yes. The password reset flow works on any device with a web browser — desktop, tablet, or mobile.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I still can't access my account after resetting?">
    Contact your property administrator or the [SUITEe support team](https://suite-e.com/contact-us/). They can verify your account status and help you regain access.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
