| **Infrastructure** | Multiple disconnected systems | A unified ecosystem — software and hardware designed to work seamlessly together |

## Benefits by role

### For property owners and managers

- **Slash staff costs** — Eliminate the need for 24/7 front desk coverage, especially expensive night shifts
- **Reduce energy bills** — Smart sensors and automated climate control prevent energy waste in unoccupied rooms
- **Scale effortlessly** — Manage multiple properties from a single dashboard without being physically present
- **Modernise without renovation** — SUITEe's retrofit devices install over existing hardware in about an hour per room

### For operations and front desk staff

- **Less manual work** — Access codes, check-in instructions, and room preparation all happen automatically
- **Real-time visibility** — The dashboard shows room status, device health, and alerts at a glance
- **Proactive alerts** — Get notified about low batteries, offline devices, or unusual access before they become problems
- **Easy guest support** — If a guest has an issue, generate a new access code or adjust settings from the dashboard in seconds

### For guests

- **Arrive anytime** — No need to coordinate with reception hours; check in at 2 AM if you want
- **No app download** — The Guest Web App works directly in the phone's browser
- **Room comfort on arrival** — The room is pre-heated or pre-cooled before you walk in
- **Control from your phone** — Adjust temperature, lock/unlock doors, and access amenities from the Guest Web App
- **Hassle-free departure** — Just leave; access expires automatically at check-out time

## Energy savings in detail

SUITEe's energy automation works across five scenarios:

| Scenario | What Happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Room booked, guest not arrived** | Room stays in Eco Mode; pre-heats/cools shortly before check-in |
| **Guest in the room** | Room operates in Guest Mode (comfortable temperature) |
| **Guest leaves temporarily** | Sensors detect vacancy → heating/cooling reduces after a set time |
| **Guest opens a window** | Window sensor detected → heating/cooling pauses to avoid waste |
| **Guest checks out** | Room immediately switches to Eco Mode until the next guest |

<Tip>
  Energy savings compound across your entire property. The more rooms you have, the greater the impact on your utility bills — especially during seasons when rooms aren't at full occupancy.
</Tip>
