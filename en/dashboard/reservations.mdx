Reservations in SUITEe are **automatically synced from your PMS** (Property Management System). You generally do not need to create reservations manually — the data flows in automatically from your booking system.

<Info>
  SUITEe is designed to work **alongside** your PMS, not replace it. All bookings, modifications, and cancellations happen in your PMS first, and SUITEe syncs automatically.
</Info>

## How reservations get into SUITEe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Booking is made">
    A guest books through any channel — your website, Booking.com, Airbnb, a travel agent, or a walk-in. The booking is recorded in your PMS.
  </Step>

  <Step title="PMS sends data to SUITEe">
    Your PMS automatically sends the booking details to SUITEe:
    - Guest name and contact details
    - Room/unit assignment
    - Check-in date and time
    - Check-out date and time
    - Reservation status (confirmed, modified, cancelled)
  </Step>

  <Step title="SUITEe creates the reservation">
    SUITEe creates a reservation record and begins preparing:
    - Digital access credentials (PIN code or mobile key) are generated
    - Room climate preparation is scheduled
    - Check-in instructions are queued for the guest
  </Step>

  <Step title="Access is activated at check-in time">
    When the guest's check-in time arrives, their access credentials become active and they receive their check-in instructions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Access expires at check-out">
    At the scheduled check-out time, the guest's access automatically expires. No manual action needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Viewing reservations

The Reservations section organises bookings into three categories:

| Category | What It Shows |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Upcoming Reservations** | Guests who will arrive in the coming days |
| **Current Reservations** | Guests who are currently checked in and staying |
| **Past Reservations** | Completed stays — available for reference and reporting |

### Reservation details

For each reservation, you can view:

| Field | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Guest Name** | The name of the guest (from PMS) |
| **Room/Unit** | Which room or apartment is assigned |
| **Check-in Date & Time** | When the guest is expected to arrive |
| **Check-out Date & Time** | When the guest is expected to depart |
| **Access Status** | Whether the digital key/PIN has been generated and sent |
| **Reservation Status** | Confirmed, checked-in, checked-out, or cancelled |

## What happens when reservations change

SUITEe handles reservation changes automatically:

| Change in PMS | What SUITEe Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Reservation modified** (dates changed) | Access credentials are updated to match new dates |
| **Room changed** | Access is moved to the new room automatically |
| **Reservation cancelled** | Access credentials are revoked immediately |
| **Guest name updated** | Guest information is updated in SUITEe |
| **New booking added** | New reservation record is created with fresh access credentials |

<Warning>
  **Important:** Always make reservation changes in your **PMS first**. SUITEe syncs from the PMS — do not try to change booking dates or room assignments directly in SUITEe. The PMS is the source of truth.
</Warning>

## Access status tracking

One of the most important fields in the reservation view is the **Access Status**, which tells you whether the guest can access their room:

| Access Status | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pending** | Access credentials have been created but are not yet active (before check-in time) |
| **Active** | Guest can currently open their door — credentials are live |
| **Expired** | Check-out time has passed — credentials are no longer valid |
| **Revoked** | Access was manually revoked by staff |

<Tip>
  If a guest contacts you saying they can't access their room, the **first thing to check** is the Access Status in the reservation details. If it shows "Pending", their check-in time may not have arrived yet.
</Tip>

## Manual actions

While most reservation management is automated, staff can perform certain manual actions when needed:

- **Resend access credentials** — If a guest didn't receive their access information, you can resend it
- **Generate a new PIN code** — If a guest forgot their PIN, generate a fresh one
- **Extend access** — If a guest's stay is extended, update it in the PMS and SUITEe will sync
- **Revoke access** — Immediately disable a guest's access in emergency situations
