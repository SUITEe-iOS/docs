The **Email Services** page (labeled **Email-Service / E-Mail** in the platform) is SUITEe's automated guest messaging and transactional email engine. It enables property managers to design standardized email templates, schedule automated dispatches based on reservation milestones (pre-arrival, check-in, check-out), and monitor automated mailing statuses.

Navigate to this page at:

```
https://hotel.suite-e.com/email-service/email
```

### Status filter tabs

Directly above the table, three filter tabs allow you to organize templates by their automation status:

| Tab | Count | Description |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Alle** | `(0)` | Displays the complete catalog of email templates created for the property, regardless of whether they are active or disabled. |
| **Aktiv** | `(0)` | Filters for templates currently enabled in the automated dispatch pipeline. When a reservation trigger occurs, active templates are sent automatically. |
| **Inaktiv** | `(0)` | Filters for drafts, paused templates, or seasonal promotions that are temporarily disabled from automatic delivery. |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/email-template-unsaved-modal.png" alt="Ungespeicherte Anderungen modal warning that changes will be lost, with Abbrechen and red Bestatigen buttons" />

- **Warning Prompt**: *Sie haben ungespeicherte Änderungen. Wirklich verlassen? Ihre Änderungen gehen verloren.* (You have unsaved changes. Really leave? Your changes will be lost).

### Left column — Automation triggers and scheduling

The left panel configures when and under what circumstances the automated email is sent:

- **Template Name (`Vorlagenname`)**: Internal name for organizing templates (e.g. `z. B. Willkommens-E-Mail nach Buchung`).
- **Subject Line (`Betreff`)**: The email subject visible to the guest in their inbox (e.g. `Ihr Aufenthalt bei {property_name}`). Includes an indicator for recommended character length (`0 / 60 Zeichen`).
- **Trigger Event (`Auslöser-Ereignis`)**: The milestone on the reservation lifecycle that activates this email:
  - **`Buchungsbestätigung`**: Fired when a new reservation is confirmed in [Booking](/dashboard/booking).
  - Additional events include guest check-in, check-out, and pre-arrival windows.
- **Trigger Action (`Auslöser-Aktion`)**: Defines relative timing:
  - **`Nach` (After)**: Delivers the email after the event occurs (e.g. 1 hour after check-in).
  - **`Vor` (Before)**: Delivers the email prior to the event (e.g. 24 hours before arrival).
- **Trigger Time (`Auslöser-Zeit`)**: A clock-picker field where you specify the exact hours and minutes of the offset.
- **Helper Callout**: An informative callout dynamically summarizes your schedule: *Legen Sie eine Auslöserzeit fest, um diese E-Mail Nach Buchungsbestätigung zu planen.*

---

### Center column — Visual email canvas (WYSIWYG)

The center panel features a rich-text visual HTML email designer built on the TinyMCE framework:

- **Formatting Toolbar**:
  - Text formatting: Headings (`Paragraph`, `H1`, `H2`), **Bold**, *Italic*, Text Color.
  - Alignment: Left, Center, Right, Justified.
  - Structure: Bulleted lists, numbered lists, indentation controls.
  - Rich Media: Insert responsive images, tables, emojis, and hyperlinks.
  - Developer Tools: Source code view (`<>`), preview render (`👁`), and visual guidelines.
- **Branded Email Layout**:
  - Pre-styled with the official **`SUITEe`** header logo.
  - Customizable content container (*Add your email content here*) where you can write your message, insert dynamic door access credentials, and embed check-in buttons.
- **Status Bar**: Displays word count and shortcut assistance (*Press Alt+0 for help*).

---

### Right column — Dynamic personalization tokens (`Verfügbare Variablen`)

The right sidebar provides a library of dynamic merge tags that automatically pull live guest and reservation data at the exact moment the email is dispatched:

<Info>
  **How to insert variables**: You can type `{` anywhere inside the email editor to open an autocomplete dropdown of available tokens, or click directly on any variable in the right sidebar to insert it at your current cursor position.
</Info>

#### Guest tokens (`GAST`)

| Variable Token | Placeholder Tag | Example Output | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vorname des Gastes** | `{vorname}` | *Max* | Injects the guest's first name for personal greetings (e.g. *Hallo {vorname}*). |
| **Nachname des Gastes** | `{nachname}` | *Mustermann* | Injects the guest's last name for formal correspondence. |
| **E-Mail-Adresse des Gastes** | `{gast_email}` | *john.doe@example.com* | Inserts the primary contact email registered on the reservation. |
| **Telefonnummer des Gastes** | `{gast_telefon}` | *+49 170 1234567* | Injects the verified phone number associated with the booking. |

## Interconnected dashboard modules

Email Services connects directly with key operational workflows across the platform:

- **[Booking](/dashboard/booking)**: Reservation milestones (confirmed booking, digital check-in, check-out) serve as the primary triggers that automatically fire email templates.
- **[Access Management](/dashboard/access-management)**: Door access PINs and digital keycard links generated in Access Management are dynamically merged into pre-arrival emails for contactless check-in.
- **[Rooms](/dashboard/rooms)**: Room names and unit details configured in the Rooms module populate personalized template placeholders.
- **[Dashboard Overview](/dashboard/overview)**: Summary metrics on guest activity and communication link back to the main control hub.
