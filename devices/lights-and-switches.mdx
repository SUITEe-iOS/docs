Light and switch modules are smart replacements for traditional light switches and power outlets. They enable remote control and automation of room lighting and other electrical fixtures.

## What are light & switch modules?

SUITEe's smart switch modules are devices that:
- **Replace traditional light switches** in guest rooms and common areas
- Allow **remote control** of room lighting from the dashboard
- Can be **automated** (e.g., lights off when room is vacant)
- Let **guests control lights** via the Guest Web App on their phone

---

## How they work

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Remote control** | Staff can turn lights on/off from the dashboard for any room |
| **Guest control** | Guests can control lighting from the Guest Web App |
| **Automation** | Lights automatically turn off when the occupancy sensor detects an empty room |
| **Physical control** | The switch still works as a normal light switch — guests can use it manually too |
| **Scheduling** | Lights can be set to turn on/off at specific times (e.g., hallway lights at night) |

---

## Automation scenarios

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Guest checks out → Lights off">
    When a guest checks out, all lights in the room are **automatically turned off** to save energy. No one needs to go room by room switching off lights.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Room vacant for extended period → Lights off">
    If the occupancy sensor detects the room has been empty for a set period (e.g., 30 minutes), lights are dimmed or turned off automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Pre-arrival welcome lighting">
    Before a guest arrives, specific lights can be turned on to create a **welcoming atmosphere** — entry light, bedside lamp, or bathroom light.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Common area scheduling">
    Hallway, lobby, and exterior lights can be scheduled to turn on and off at specific times (e.g., corridor lights dim after midnight).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Installation

Light and switch modules are designed for **easy retrofit**:

- They fit into **standard electrical boxes** used by traditional switches
- A qualified electrician can swap them out quickly
- No new wiring is needed — they use existing electrical connections
- Once installed, they connect to the system through the gateway

<Info>
  Like all SUITEe devices, light modules are **pre-configured** before shipping. Once physically installed, they automatically appear in the dashboard and can be assigned to rooms.
</Info>
