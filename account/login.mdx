The SUITEe admin dashboard is where property managers and staff monitor and control everything about their property. Here's how to access it.

## What you need

Before logging in, make sure you have:
- Your **email address** registered with SUITEe
- Your **password** (provided by the SUITEe team or your property administrator)
- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge)

<Warning>
  If you're logging in for the **first time**, your credentials will have been provided by the SUITEe team or your property administrator. If you haven't received them, contact your manager or [SUITEe support](https://suite-e.com/contact-us/).
</Warning>

## Login steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the platform">
    Open your web browser and go to:

    ```
    https://hotel.suite-e.com/auth
    ```

    You will see the SUITEe login page with the logo, email field, password field, and a **"Log In"** (or **"Anmelden"**) button.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/login-german.png" alt="SUITEe German login page with email and password fields" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your email address">
    Click on the **email field** and type the email address associated with your SUITEe account.

    <Info>
      This is the same email address the SUITEe team or your property administrator used to set up your account. It is usually your work email.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your password">
    Click on the **password field** and type your password. The password is hidden by default (shown as dots). You may see an "eye" icon to toggle password visibility.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click 'Log In'">
    Click the **"Log In"** (or **"Anmelden"**) button. If your credentials are correct, you will either:
    - Be taken directly to the **Admin Dashboard**, or
    - Be prompted for **OTP verification** (see [OTP Verification](/account/otp-verification))
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Common login issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I can't remember my password">
    Click the **"Forgot Password?"** link on the login page to reset it. See the [Forgot Password](/account/forgot-password) guide for detailed steps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm entering the correct password but login fails">
    - Make sure you're using the correct email address
    - Check that Caps Lock is not accidentally enabled
    - Try clearing your browser cache or using an incognito/private window
    - If the problem persists, contact your administrator to confirm your account is active
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I see an error message after clicking Log In">
    Common error messages include:
    - **"Invalid credentials"** — Your email or password is incorrect
    - **"Account locked"** — Too many failed attempts; wait a few minutes or contact support
    - **"Session expired"** — Your previous session timed out; simply log in again
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  **Pro tip:** Bookmark `https://hotel.suite-e.com` in your browser for quick access to the SUITEe dashboard.
</Tip>
